Across browsers

The extension runs from the same folder in every browser. Your settings live in the ayarlar.json file in that folder: add a term in one browser and the other picks it up within a minute or two.

  1. Click "Link the folder" and choose the vi-spoiler-kalkani folder you loaded the extension from.
  2. If the browser asks for edit permission, choose "Allow on every visit".
  3. Do this once in every browser you use the extension in.

What it can miss

No extension catches everything. Here is exactly what this one can miss, so you can decide with your eyes open.

Open the setup wizard again

  • Images without textA screenshot with no title or caption is not recognised unless it copies an official image.
  • Inside videosFrames and audio are not scanned. A video can play if its title and description give nothing away.
  • New tab and address barEdge and Brave new-tab news and address bar suggestions are closed to extensions. Turn them off in the browser settings.
  • Private windowsPrivate windows are not protected unless "Allow in Incognito" is on for the extension.
