VI Spoiler Shield hides news, images and videos about the game before you see them. Setup takes two minutes.

  • Text is hidden before it reaches the screen.
  • Copies of official images are recognised by fingerprint.
  • YouTube videos do not play until their title is checked.

Pin it to the toolbar

Keep the icon in view to see how much was hidden and to manage a site in one click.

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of the address bar.
  2. Click the pin next to VI Spoiler Shield.

Protect private windows too

Browsers turn extensions off in private windows by default, and that is where spoilers leak most.

  1. Open the extension's settings page with the button below.
  2. Turn on "Allow in Incognito".

How strict?

You can change this any time from the extension icon.

Same settings in every browser

The extension runs from the same folder in every browser. Link this browser to that folder and the terms and settings you add reach your other browsers too.

In the window that opens, choose the vi-spoiler-shield folder. If asked for edit permission, choose "Allow on every visit".

Folder linked.

Turn off new-tab news

The browser's own new-tab page and address bar suggestions are closed to extensions. Turn them off once so nothing leaks from there.
